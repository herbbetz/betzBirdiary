The weight signal is shown in blue. The offset drift signal in orange is the reference signal. It is updated after a timeout to reset the weight to zero for the supposedly unloaded load cell. Sigma in red is the standard deviation of the weight signal's jitter around zero (baseline). Threshold in green is slightly adapted to Sigma (noise). The vertical axis is logarithmic, with a range of ±250 grams. The horizontal axis shows the last 60 seconds of data.
